Style recipes as looping mini-Lenses in a horizontal carousel.

```jsx
<div className="el-carousel">
  <StyleCard name="Bedtime warm" tag="my style" scene="nightstand" grade="warm" />
  <StyleCard name="Punch-in zoom" tag="instant" anim="punch" selected />
  <StyleCard name="Whip pan" tag="generates new shots" anim="whip" scene="gym" />
</div>
```

- Each card loops its recipe on real footage: punch, whip, ramp, crash, jump.
- **My styles** always come first, tagged "my style". "Save as style" in the context menu adds one.
- Under the carousel, one quiet learned line: "you usually punch in on the hook · apply?".
- Selecting previews it live in the main Lens; Apply commits to this beat or all beats.
