The empty state is an idea engine: blank paper, one glass prompt "What's the idea?", answers land on the canvas.

```jsx
<IdeaCard kicker="angle 1 · objection first" line="“I thought a smart ring would be uncomfortable to sleep in.”" why="names the doubt before the viewer can" />
<GhostCard label="hook" />
```

- Answers arrive in this order: **hook critique** (one line), **3 sharper angle cards**, a **ghost beat-sheet row**, **risk flags** (amber dot + one line).
- Picking an angle fills the ghost row with its beats; dropping footage on a ghost card fills it.
- Raised paper cards, no borders. Ghost cards are dashed outlines in the true 9:16 shape.
